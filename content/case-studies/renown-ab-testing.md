## The situation

Renown had no A/B testing in place. The team made changes to their games and hoped for the best, without a reliable way to know whether a change had improved things. With five released mobile games, that meant a lot of decisions about monetization, onboarding and difficulty were made without evidence.

## What I did

I built the experimentation foundation across all of Renown's released games:

- **Firebase setup** in each game
- **Event tracking**, so there was data to measure results against
- **Remote Config**, so changes could be tested and rolled out without shipping a new build
- **A/B experiments** covering monetization, onboarding and game difficulty

The five games are each released on Google Play and the Apple App Store, and three are also on the Amazon Appstore. Because I worked on the team as both engineer and analyst, I also analyzed engagement and monetization data, and helped the team interpret results and decide what to do with them.

## The result

- **Coverage:** every released Renown game now has A/B testing available, on each store the game is released on.
- **Better decisions:** test results changed some of the team's decisions, and informed their choices on direction. They also gave the team a clearer picture of what works and what doesn't.
