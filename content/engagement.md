I work with clients as a fractional or contract data analyst and data engineer. There's no standard package: the size and length of an engagement depend on what you need.

## Fractional

An ongoing, part-time role on your team. You get a data analyst or engineer for the hours you need without hiring someone full time. This suits teams with steady data work but not enough of it to fill a full-time position, or teams that want to build out data capability gradually.

## Contract

Work with a defined scope or a set term, such as building a pipeline, designing a warehouse, delivering a set of dashboards, or running an analysis. This suits a specific need with a clear outcome.

## Hours and duration

I don't have fixed hours or lengths. I'm open to whatever fits the work, from a short project to an ongoing arrangement.

## Pricing

I bill hourly or on a retainer, depending on which suits the work. We'll agree on the structure and rate together once we've talked through what you need.

## How I work

I work mostly remotely and can join occasional on-site sessions in the Vancouver area.

## Getting started

It starts with an intro call, where we talk through what you're trying to do and whether I can help.
