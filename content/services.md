I take on analysis, engineering, BI and experimentation work, either as a single project or as an ongoing fractional role. [See how engagements work](#engagement).

## Data analysis

Ad-hoc and ongoing analysis in SQL and Python. I take a business question, find the data that answers it, and explain the result in terms the people making the decision can use. That includes cleaning up inconsistent data and building reports you can rerun.

Case study: [Modo Carshare](modo-carshare)

## Data engineering and pipeline development

I design and build the infrastructure analytics depends on: ETL/ELT pipelines, data lakes and data warehouses, documented so your team can maintain them. My cloud work has been on AWS and GCP.

## BI and dashboards

I help you choose a BI tool that fits your team and budget, then build the dashboards that make it useful. My tool experience includes Power BI, Tableau, Looker Studio and custom Plotly-Dash apps.

Case studies: [Modo Carshare](modo-carshare), [movmi mapping dashboard](movmi-mapping-dashboard)

## Experimentation and A/B testing

I set up the analytics and experimentation foundation for product teams: event tracking, remote configuration and A/B tests, using Firebase.

Case study: [Renown Entertainment](renown-ab-testing)
