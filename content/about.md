I've always been fascinated by how people interact with technology: how an interface can open up something a person couldn't do on their own. That curiosity took me through more than a decade in the game industry as a UI/UX designer and developer, shipping titles on console, mobile and VR/AR, and eventually leading a team of nine as product owner.

Along the way I found that data systems are another kind of interface. A good pipeline, warehouse or dashboard gives a team clarity about what's happening and what to do next, the same way a good UI does for a player. That pulled me into data analysis and engineering, and since 2020 it's been the core of my work: building analytics for shared mobility operators and mobile game studios, in SQL, Python and the cloud.

Because I've worked on both sides, I can move between the technical work and the people who rely on it. I'm as comfortable writing the pipeline as explaining the result to someone who has never opened a query editor.

I'm a lifelong learner, and I'd rather learn a new tool for the job than force the old one to fit. My background is a BA in Cognitive Science from Simon Fraser University, a Data Engineering Nanodegree from Udacity (2020) and an AI Essentials Data Fellowship at SFU (2021).

Solid State Entity is the consulting practice I run. It's just me, so the person you talk to is the person who does the work.

If you have data that should be working harder for your team, I'd like to hear about it.
