They're where I have the most direct experience, but the work (pipelines, warehouses, dashboards, experimentation) applies to any team with data that needs to be more reliable and useful.

If your industry isn't listed here, the work is the same: understand the question, get the data in order, and make it usable.

## Transportation

Since 2020 I've done data work in shared mobility and carshare, through the consulting firm movmi and, since 2021, through Solid State Entity. Projects included a data warehouse and Power BI dashboards for Modo Carshare, and a mapping dashboard reused across many of movmi's clients.

Transportation data comes from vehicles, bookings, locations and operations, and it's often spread across systems that were never designed to work together. I know how to bring those sources together so operators can see what's happening across their fleet.

Case studies: [Modo Carshare](modo-carshare), [movmi mapping dashboard](movmi-mapping-dashboard)

## Video games

I spent over ten years inside the game industry as a UI/UX designer and developer. I'm not claiming a decade as a game analyst. What that time gave me is a first-hand understanding of player behavior, game funnels, KPIs and monetization, so I know which questions are worth asking and what the numbers mean. Titles I've worked on include Metroid Prime: Federation Force (Nintendo 3DS), Tron: Evolution (Xbox 360, PS3, PC) and Hidden Agenda (Facebook).

On the analytics side, I integrated Firebase A/B testing across all released mobile games at Renown Entertainment.

Case study: [Renown Entertainment](renown-ab-testing)
